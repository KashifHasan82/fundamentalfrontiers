Scenario cards

Start with one loss event such as repeat defects, late supplier delivery, or audit finding recurrence.

  • 90 day timeline
  • Constraints built in
  • Clear goal state

Role cards

Assign accountability across process owner, auditor, operations, and leadership to surface handoff risk.

  • Decision rights
  • Evidence owners
  • Escalation paths

Decision cards

Teams choose between actions such as approve supplier change, delay, add controls, or increase monitoring.

  • Tradeoffs visible
  • Time and cost pressure
  • Short cycle learning

Control cards

Controls are specific actions such as verification steps, segregation of duties, change control, CAPA discipline.

  • Prevent recurrence
  • Reduce exceptions
  • Clarify ownership

Evidence cards

Evidence is designed to be maintainable, audit ready, and tied to the control, not to paperwork volume.

  • Audit trail
  • Interview readiness
  • Documentation discipline

Outcome tracking

Track risk, recurrence, cost, and evidence readiness over time and use it to verify controls are stable.

  • Risk trending
  • Recurrence trending
  • Audit readiness
