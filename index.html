How the sprint works

Simple structure, clear timeline, practical delivery

Each week ends with decisions, actions, and evidence your team can maintain.

1
Rapid gap scan
Current state versus required controls, prioritize the top gaps.
2
Evidence and SOP alignment
Update key procedures, forms, and records for clarity and traceability.
3
Fix and verify
Close gaps, test the control, and confirm it works in real use.
4
Mock audit and closeout
Walkthrough, findings log, readiness summary, and sustain plan.
