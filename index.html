How it works
A simple 4 step structure
Designed for speed, clarity, and control. Each step creates a deliverable the team can keep using.
1
Rapid gap scan
Current state vs required controls. Prioritize top gaps with owners.
2
Evidence and SOP alignment
Update procedures, checklists, and records for traceability and clarity.
3
Fix and verify
Close gaps, test the control, verify operation in real use.
4
Mock audit and closeout
Walkthrough, findings log, readiness summary, sustain cadence.
Deliverables
Concrete outputs your team can run
Focused documents and templates. No heavy decks required. Each output is designed to be used, owned, and maintained.
Output details
Practical, ready to run artifacts
Readiness gap map

A prioritized gap list with named owners, target dates, and the exact proof needed to close each gap. This becomes the single control board for the sprint.

  • Requirement mapped to current state and risk
  • Owner, due date, and escalation route
  • Single link to the evidence location
Evidence pack

Organized proof set aligned to controls and audit needs, with a clear file trail and ownership. Built for fast retrieval when pressure is high.

  • Evidence index aligned to controls
  • Version and approval trail where needed
  • Fast retrieval and clear ownership
SOP and checklist updates

Top procedures and forms updated for clarity, traceability, and consistent execution. The goal is fewer exceptions and cleaner proof in daily work.

  • Critical steps made explicit and testable
  • Records designed to produce proof by default
  • Training signoff aligned to real execution
Mock audit report

A walkthrough output and findings log that shows what holds, what fails, and what must be fixed before the audit. Designed to drive action, not debate.

  • Findings with severity and owner
  • Required evidence per finding
  • Readiness summary and closeout actions
CAPA log and closure rules

Actions close only when effectiveness is verified over time. This prevents repeat findings and the cycle of reopening the same issues.

  • Root cause and corrective actions
  • Effectiveness checks defined and dated
  • Closure rule that prevents recurrence
Sustain cadence

A simple routine to prevent drift after the sprint, sized for normal operating constraints. The intent is stability that survives real workloads.

  • 30 day stabilization checks
  • 60 day effectiveness review
  • 90 day internal audit and refresh
