Why this program

Supplier disruption is usually concentrated in a few suppliers

This program is designed to stabilize delivery and quality fast by focusing effort where it has the highest impact. The goal is not only escalation. The goal is stable performance supported by simple controls and evidence.

On time delivery improves

Target late suppliers with a weekly recovery cadence, actions, owners, and escalation paths.

Quality escapes reduce

Containment, verification, and CAPA discipline tied to repeat failure modes.

Expedite cost reduces

Fewer exceptions means less premium freight, firefighting, and rework.

How the sprint works

Simple structure, clear timeline, practical delivery

Run the sprint as a management system. Each week ends with decisions, actions, and evidence that holds.

1
Supplier triage
Rank suppliers by delivery risk, defects, and disruption impact.
2
Root cause focus
Isolate the top recurring issues and failure modes per supplier.
3
Recovery actions
Implement containment, verification, and CAPA commitments.
4
Weekly governance
Cadence, owners, escalation triggers, and decision discipline.
5
Sustain and prove
Track metrics and keep controls stable with evidence over time.
What you get

Concrete deliverables that teams can run without consultants

Deliverables are designed for clarity, ownership, and continuity. Not large slide decks.

CAPA evidence trail

Outcome tracking metrics
